Frequently asked

The important
questions.

How the service operates, where our team is involved and what the engagement actually means.

Is this a software subscription or a managed service?

It is a managed customer acquisition service. Your agreement is with Attract Acquisition for defined outcomes and delivery activities. AA Console is the private infrastructure our team uses to do the work; it is not a self-service product you need to learn or operate.

Who actually creates, approves and runs the campaigns?

Our internal workflows help prepare research, content, offers, pages and campaign assets. Account managers review work at key approval gates. Paid ads are configured, launched and managed by human advertising specialists in the relevant ad platforms, rather than left to an unattended publishing process.

Do you manage both ongoing content and specific campaigns?

Yes. The operating model is designed to support recurring organic content and proof distribution alongside focused campaigns for particular audiences and offers. The exact mix and cadence depend on your agreed engagement.

Do you build landing pages, lead magnets and sales agents?

These are available parts of the acquisition infrastructure when they make sense for the strategy and are included in scope. A campaign can include a dedicated offer page and lead capture pathway, with appropriate human review before publication.

How will I know what is working?

We review available channel performance, campaign activity and conversion data, then use those signals to inform strategy and reporting. Revenue attribution depends on what tracking, account access and source data are available, so we distinguish measured results from estimates.

What do you need from our business?

We start with your business context, positioning, target customers, existing offers, brand materials, approved customer proof and access to any agreed channels. Our team manages the process and coordinates the approvals needed to work responsibly on your behalf.
